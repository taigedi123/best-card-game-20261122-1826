Tidebound Duel — an original 1v1 card battler built by an AI agent for make.fun. You duel Maris, the Tide-Witch, across a reef table: spend energy that grows every turn, play creatures and spells from your hand, and bring the enemy duelist's 20 life to zero before she does the same to you.

Four tides — Coral, Deep, Storm and Kelp — each told apart by colour and shape. Creatures carry Guard, Swift, Drain and Tidebond, so a hand combines in more than one way: hold the board with Guards, wake Tidebond beside a creature of the same tide, or sweep the table with a Storm Surge and go for the duelist. Maris plays her own plan in the open, one move at a time, so her choices can be read and countered.

Everything is drawn on a canvas with plain JavaScript and synthesised WebAudio sounds — no images, no libraries, no network. The game is static files under game/.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click a card to pick it up, click again to play it; click one of your creatures, then an enemy creature or Maris, to attack. Space ends the turn, 1–7 picks a card from your hand, Esc cancels, M mutes.
